or a signature alone does not hold the room. We will send you an explicit
confirmation. Until you have it, treat the date as unheld.

## 5. Hours

- **Monday to Friday:** quiet setup from 3:00 p.m., no amplified sound before
  5:00 p.m., guest events 5:00 to 11:00 p.m., teardown finished by 1:00 a.m.
- **Saturday:** guest events 8:00 a.m. to midnight, teardown finished by
  2:00 a.m.
- **Sunday:** guest events 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., teardown finished by
  midnight.

Exceptions and same-day extensions need our approval and an open schedule.
Approved overtime is billed at $175 per full hour. We do not bill part hours.

## 6. Setup, equipment, and extra help

Included: up to 30 tables, 200 chairs, the built-in stage, available
microphones, speakers, projector and screens, guest wifi, the lounge area,
shared onsite parking, and ground-floor access. The kitchen is not included and
may not be used.

Unless you purchase setup and reset service, you configure the room and return
tables and chairs to the staging area. You or your caterer remove all trash,
decorations, food, personal property, and vendor equipment.

Available to purchase:

- Table and chair setup and reset: $250 up to 75 guests, $400 for 76 to 150,
  $550 for 151 to 200.
- A/V technician: $75 per hour, two hour minimum.
- Additional event staff: $125 per hour per person, two hour minimum.
- Extras ordered fewer than seven days before your event carry a 20% rush fee.
- Linens, decor, event lighting, extra equipment, catering, and specialty
  staging are quoted separately.

## 7. Cleaning, damage, and your security deposit

The $250 cleaning fee covers routine cleaning after your event. Extraordinary
cleaning, damage, missing equipment, false-alarm costs, and unapproved overtime
may be deducted from your $500 security deposit. We will give you an itemized
notice within five business days.

Anything eligible is returned to your original payment method within seven
business days after your event and our inspection. Amounts beyond the security
deposit remain your responsibility and we may invoice them.

Property left behind is held for seven days, after which we may donate or
dispose of it.

## 8. Vendors, food, and alcohol

All vendors need our approval in advance. Outside caterers must be licensed and
insured. Food may be assembled onsite. Cooking and kitchen use are not allowed.
You are responsible for your vendors' conduct, damage, cleanup, and compliance.

Alcohol requires our written approval and must be supplied and served by an
approved, licensed, insured provider. You may not bring your own. Alcohol
service ends 30 minutes before your event ends, and alcohol events require
security that you pay for.

Your alcohol plan, security arrangements, and running schedule are due 14 days
before your event. Changes to any of those after that deadline need our written
approval.

## 9. Insurance and security

We may require a certificate of insurance depending on the risk of your event,
starting at $1 million per occurrence naming Anata as an additional insured.

Default security staffing is one guard for 1 to 75 guests, two for 76 to 150,
and three for 151 to 200. We may require more based on alcohol, format,
performers, ticketing, the age mix of guests, or prior incidents. Security is
billed at provider cost plus a 20% coordination fee.

## 10. Decorations, conduct, and safety

Painter's tape and removable adhesive strips are fine. Balloons, confetti,
glitter, flower petals, rice, and artificial snow are allowed only if you remove
them completely; residue results in an extraordinary cleaning charge.

Open flames are prohibited. Smoke and haze effects need prior approval. Smoking
and vaping are prohibited indoors and within 20 feet of any entrance. Cannabis
and illegal substances are prohibited. Please do not move Anata furniture or
equipment without our consent.

We may stop any activity that is unsafe, illegal, destructive, or materially
disruptive, without refund. Teen events require you or another approved
responsible adult onsite throughout, and children must stay supervised.

## 11. Parking, recordings, and your information

Onsite parking is shared, not exclusive, and not guaranteed. Roughly 50 spaces
may be available depending on other building use. No overnight parking. The
nearby North Lehi park-and-ride may be used for overflow, and guests park at
their own risk.

We will only photograph or record your event for marketing with your separate
written consent. Security cameras operate in common and event areas, never in
restrooms or private areas. We retain signed agreements, payment records,
incident reports, inspection evidence, and consent records for seven years.

## 12. Responsibility

You are responsible for your guests, your vendors, and anyone you bring into the
building, and for any injury or damage they cause. You use the space and its
equipment at your own risk.

Except where the law does not allow it, Anata's total liability under this
agreement is limited to the amount you paid for the booking, and neither party
is liable for indirect or consequential losses.
