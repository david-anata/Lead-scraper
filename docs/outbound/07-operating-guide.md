# Outbound Operating Guide

How Anata's outbound machine gets run, week to week. One page. Pin it.

## Who does what
- **David:** runs our Clay table, pulls the list, loads Instantly, owns the target,
  the offer, and the numbers. (The agency runs their own separate table; ours is ours.)
- **David + Gabe:** work the Instantly replies and book the calls. (Grows to more people
  as volume climbs.)
- **The app:** finds and filters the brands, remembers who's been contacted, and keeps
  the scoreboard. It never sends or moves money.

## The weekly rhythm (Monday to Friday)

**Monday - pull and load.** David downloads the brand list from the app, imports it into
the Clay table, and lets Clay find the contacts, verify emails, qualify, and personalize.
The good ones flow to Instantly, which sends across the week from the warmed domains.

**Tuesday to Friday - work the replies.** David and Gabe watch the Instantly inbox and
reply fast to anyone interested (minutes, not hours), book the call, and tag them. Replies
are handled inside Instantly, never forwarded out.

**Friday - review.** David checks the scoreboard: reply rate, positive replies, sending
health, and booked calls. Decide which niche is winning and pour more into it, and what
copy to test next.

## The dials to watch
Inbox health (keep high), reply rate and positive-reply rate (is the copy working), bounce
rate (keep low), booked calls per week (the goal, target 15+), emails per booked call
(efficiency). All on the scoreboard.

## The guardrails (always on)
- Daily send cap, starts low and ramps.
- The app never lets a brand be emailed twice.
- Nothing sends until David approves the copy and sees a real list.
- Sending auto-pauses if inbox health dips, then recovers.
- Block list for clients, competitors, and the agency's brands.

## When to scale
Only push more volume once deliverability is healthy AND David and Gabe can keep up with
replies. Reply capacity is the real limit, not sending.
