# Arena tax determination — request to send an accountant

Prepared: 2026-07-31
Purpose: obtain the written determination that unblocks Arena rate-plan approval
Blocks: `tax_treatment` decision, and through it `effective_date` and launch

The request below is written to be sent as-is. It asks for a determination, not
advice, because the launch register requires recorded evidence: a jurisdiction,
a numeric rate, an effective date, a category mapping, and a named firm.

---

## The email

**Subject:** Utah sales tax determination for event venue rental — written memo requested

Hello,

We operate The Anata Building at 1657 N. State Street, Lehi, Utah 84043. We are
about to begin renting an event space to customers and need a written
determination of our Utah sales tax obligations before we publish prices or
sign our first contract.

We are asking for a short written memo we can keep on file, not a verbal answer.
Our booking system will not let us approve a price list until the determination
is recorded.

**The business**

We rent a 6,000 square foot event venue called The Arena by the hour to
companies and individuals for gatherings, workshops, talks, and celebrations.
A standard booking is a six hour block. We do not serve food or drink, and we
do not provide alcohol. Customers may bring their own vendors.

**What we charge for**

Please tell us, for each of the following, whether it is taxable in Utah, and
at what combined state and local rate for our Lehi address:

1. The base venue rental charge (hourly, and the six hour block)
2. Overtime hours beyond the booked window, billed at an hourly premium
3. Setup and reset service, sold in tiers by expected attendance
4. Routine cleaning, charged as a flat fee
5. Equipment and add-on rentals provided with the room
6. A refundable security deposit that is returned when no damage occurs
7. A non-refundable booking deposit that is applied to the final balance
8. Cancellation charges retained when a customer cancels
9. Any discount we apply, specifically whether tax is calculated before or
   after the discount

**What we need in writing**

- The jurisdiction and the combined rate that applies at our address
- Which of the nine categories above are taxable and which are not
- The effective date of that rate
- Whether we must register for a Utah sales tax licence before our first
  taxable sale, and if so, the registration we need
- The filing frequency we should expect
- Any exemption we must honour, for example a non-profit or government
  customer, and the certificate we must collect and retain
- Your firm name and a reference we can cite as the source of this
  determination

**Timing**

We cannot publish final pricing or approve our contract terms until this is
recorded, so we would like the memo within two weeks if that is workable. If
anything above is unclear or you need more detail on how we bill, please ask.

Thank you,

David Narayan
Anata
david@anatainc.com

## Notes for David, not for the accountant

**You do not need this answer to go live.** The launch register accepts two
resolutions for `tax_treatment`: accountant evidence, or an explicit decision to
keep tax as quote-review-required. The second is a real, recorded decision, not
a workaround, and it unblocks the rate plan and the launch date the same way.

What it costs you: quotes cannot show a final tax-inclusive total, so the
customer sees a subtotal and a line saying tax is confirmed on the contract.
And if Utah does tax venue rental, you owe that tax on every event booked in the
meantime, out of your own margin, because you did not collect it. On events
around $1,050 to $1,300 that exposure is small per booking and grows with
volume.

**Recommendation:** record tax as quote-review-required now so The Arena can
sell, send the memo request in parallel, and switch to the recorded rate the day
it comes back. Do not let an unbooked accountant hold a finished system closed.

**Finding one:** you want a Utah CPA or a sales-tax specialist who handles
venue, hospitality, or event rental. It is a narrow question and should be one
short engagement rather than an ongoing relationship. Ask up front whether they
will put the determination in writing, because a verbal answer does not satisfy
the register.
